## About the Manelli Method

Mirella's original signature foil highlight technique. Key facts:

- Double zigzag parting (top AND bottom of section)
- Uses a Dream Weaver comb to separate mids and ends
- Creates a "broken zigzag" — mimics teasy lights without any teasing
- No shadow root needed — blend happens naturally in the foil
- Ideal for: color corrections, banding/spottiness, lived-in looks, brunette-to-blonde
- Minimal foils needed — saves significant chair time
- Result is highly diffused, soft at the root, easy to grow out
